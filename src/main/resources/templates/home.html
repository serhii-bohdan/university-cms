Welcome dear user 👋

Improve and automate your learning process using our CMS. Our platform is designed to streamline administrative tasks, enhance teaching and learning experiences, and foster meaningful communication between students, teachers, and managers. With our CMS, you can easily manage courses, track student progress, and create engaging content that meets the needs of today's learners. Join us in transforming education for a better future.

Basic features:

  1. For Manager:
    1. Manages groups in the system (create, update, delete).
    2. Add, delete, and edit information about participants in the learning process (teachers, students).
  2. For Teachers:
    1. Create and edit your courses.
    2. Add students to courses.
    3. Evaluate students by giving marks.
    4. Compose and manage your lessons schedule.
  3. For Students:
    1. Use the courses that are present on the platform.
    2. Track your performance using marks.
    3. Create and manage your schedule.
